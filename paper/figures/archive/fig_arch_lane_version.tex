\caption{\textbf{SpatialMoE-SOD.}
A PVTv2-B4 backbone produces features at three resolutions.
Each scale is handled by its \emph{own} Spatial MoE layer: a depthwise-convolutional
router turns every token into eight logits, noisy top-$K{=}2$ sends the token to two
experts, and those experts' outputs are combined with their gate weights.
Per-token routing entropy $\Hs_s$, taken over the full eight-way distribution and
normalised by $\log 8$, is added to the routed features by the entropy-fusion block.
The cross-attention decoder merges the three scales coarse-to-fine, and two
$1{\times}1$ convolutions produce the saliency and boundary maps.
Red arrows mark the refinement direction; colours are keyed by the legend.}
\label{fig:arch}
